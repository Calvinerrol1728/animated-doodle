## 4. Full Description

### Give yourself thirty quiet minutes that are only yours.

Self-care doesn't have to be complicated. Sometimes it's a quiet morning, a good night's
sleep, a walk outside, or simply giving yourself permission to rest.

**30 Days of Self-Care** is a beautifully designed printable journal that gives you a
small, gentle space to pause each day — with thoughtful prompts that help you notice what
you need, protect your energy, and speak to yourself more kindly.

No pressure. No streaks to maintain. No wrong answers.

## 7. Pricing

Printable journals sit in a **much stronger price band than children's story PDFs** —
buyers treat them as a tool, not a one-off read, and 40 pages of original design supports
a real price.

| Tier | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Launch / intro** | **$9.00** | First 1–2 weeks only, to earn first reviews |
| **Core price** | **$14.00** | ← recommended standing price |
| **Bundle** | **$24.00** | Journal + a future companion (habit tracker, gratitude pack) |

**What you keep per sale**

| Platform | Fees | Net on $14 |
|---|---|---|
| **Gumroad** | 10% + 2.9% + $0.30 | **$11.88** |
| **Etsy** | 6.5% + 3% + $0.25 + $0.20 listing | **≈ $12.22** |

Etsy nets slightly more per sale *and* brings its own buyer traffic — for a printable
like this, Etsy should be your primary channel and Gumroad your owned backup.

**Do not price this at $5.** Under-pricing a 40-page journal signals "AI filler" to the
exact buyer who would otherwise pay $14. In this category, a higher price increases
perceived quality and often increases conversion.
