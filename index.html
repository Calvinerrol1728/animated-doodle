How to Use Your Recruit Monitoring Excel Workbook & Registration Form

1. Shareable ADD Registration Form
Share the link /register with recruits. When they fill out the form, their information (Name, Contact, Recruiter, ADD Date) is automatically appended as a new row in your Excel sheet with a new Recruit ID (e.g., REC-007) and set to Pending attendance.
2. Tracking ADD Attendance (Col H)
Once the session concludes, update Attended ADD? in Column H to Yes (attended), No (missed), or Rescheduled.
3. Tracking LMS Completion (Col L)
When a recruit agrees to start online training, change Column L from Not Started to In Progress. When they finish and pass, change to Yes.
4. Automatic Pipeline Stage (Col P)
The stage tag in Column P automatically updates: ADD Scheduled ➔ ADD Attended - Awaiting LMS ➔ LMS In Progress ➔ LMS Completed (Ready for Exam).
