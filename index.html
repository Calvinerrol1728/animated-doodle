How to Use Your Recruit Monitoring Excel Workbook

Step 1: Input Complete Name (Col B)
Enter the recruit's full name in Column B. The Recruit ID (Col A) and automated pipeline status formulas will activate automatically!
Step 2: Track ADD Attendance (Col H)
ADD stands for Agency Discovery Day / Agent Development Day. Choose from the dropdown: Yes (attended), No (missed), Rescheduled, or Pending.
Step 3: Track LMS Completion (Col L)
LMS stands for Learning Management System (pre-licensing module portal). Choose: Yes (completed/certified), In Progress (currently studying), or Not Started.
Step 4: Execute Next Action & Target Follow-up (Cols Q & R)
Follow the automated Pipeline Stage (Col P) to immediately know what action is required: schedule exam, follow up on pending modules, or re-invite to ADD.
