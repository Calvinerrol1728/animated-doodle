## 4. Product Description

### He was afraid to fly. So they started with one tiny hop.

Princess Lila is exploring the castle garden when she hears a tiny sound behind a
flowering bush.

*"Achoo!"*

It's Toto — a little green dragon with his wings tucked tight, lost and far from home.
And Toto has a secret. He has wings, but he has never flown. Every time he tries, his
feet go wobbly and his heart goes nervous.

*"What if I fall?"* he asks.

So Lila doesn't ask him to soar. She asks for one tiny hop.

---

**A gentle story about the kind of bravery that starts small — and the friend who
believes in you while you find it.**

- Homeschool social-emotional learning

### 🌙 Why children connect with it

Toto doesn't conquer his fear in one heroic leap. He takes a tiny hop, lands in the
daisies, laughs, and tries again.

That's the whole lesson, and children feel it immediately: *bravery doesn't mean you
aren't scared — it means you try anyway.*

> begins with one tiny try."*
