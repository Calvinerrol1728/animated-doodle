# Princess Lila and the Little Dragon Who Was Afraid to Fly
*A Story About Courage, Friendship, and Trying Again*

Ages 3–7 · 15 story pages + cover + ending · 8.5 × 11 in portrait
Style: soft hand-painted Ghibli-inspired watercolor storybook

Narration is rendered **exactly as written** inside the cream cloud narration bubble at
the top of each page. Character dialogue goes in smaller pastel bubbles.

---

## Character reference (must stay identical on every page)

**Princess Lila** — petite childlike 7-year-old; soft round face, warm light-brown skin,
large sparkling violet eyes, small button nose, rosy cheeks, long wavy dark-brown hair
partially tied with a pale pink ribbon, small golden crown with one heart-shaped pink
jewel, soft sky-blue dress with cream Peter Pan collar and short puff sleeves, thin
golden belt, white ankle socks, soft pink shoes, small cream satchel worn across her body.

**Toto** — tiny young dragon; small round childlike body, soft mint-green scales, cream
belly, tiny rounded horns, large turquoise eyes, small rounded wings, short curly tail,
tiny lavender spikes down his back, button nostrils, expressive eyebrows, slightly
oversized head. Never sharp or frightening.

**Pippi** — tiny bluebird; small round body, sky-blue feathers, cream belly, tiny yellow
beak, round black eyes, small fluffy tail. **Only ever ONE Pippi in a scene.**

**Queen Marielle** — elegant woman early 30s; warm light-brown skin, kind brown eyes,
long dark-brown loose braided hair, small golden crown, soft peach gown, cream shawl,
delicate floral embroidery, warm motherly smile.

**Page 2 — The Kingdom on the Hill**
> In a little kingdom surrounded by green hills lived a cheerful princess named Lila. She loved exploring the castle gardens, climbing little hills, and meeting new friends. But one morning, she discovered someone who needed a little help.

**Page 3 — A Tiny Sneeze**
> Suddenly, Lila heard a tiny sound. "Achoo!" Behind the bush sat a little green dragon with his wings tucked tightly against his back. He looked at Lila with wide eyes and quickly hid his face behind his tail.
>
> *Toto:* "Achoo!"

**Page 4 — Meet Toto**
> Lila sat quietly beside him. "Hello," she said. "My name is Lila." The little dragon peeked out. "I'm Toto," he whispered. "I came from the hills, but I can't find my way home."
>
> *Lila:* "Hello! My name is Lila."
> *Toto:* "I'm Toto."

**Page 5 — Toto's Secret**
> Lila soon learned that Toto had a secret. He had tiny wings, but he had never flown. Every time he tried, his little feet became wobbly and his heart became nervous. "What if I fall?" Toto asked.
>
> *Toto:* "What if I fall?"

**Page 6 — A Little Plan**
> Lila smiled. "You don't have to fly high," she said. "We can start with one tiny hop." Toto looked at his wings. One tiny hop didn't sound quite so scary.
>
> *Lila:* "We can start with one tiny hop."

**Page 7 — The First Hop**
> Toto took a deep breath. He bent his knees, opened his little wings, and jumped. For one tiny moment, his feet lifted off the ground. Then—plop!—he landed softly in a patch of daisies.

**Page 8 — Trying Again**
> Toto looked at the daisies beneath him and giggled. "I didn't fall very far!" he said. Lila laughed. "That's because you were brave enough to try." So Toto decided to try one more time.
>
> *Toto:* "I didn't fall very far!"
> *Lila:* "That's because you were brave enough to try."

**Page 9 — A Little Higher**
> This time, Toto flapped his wings twice. He rose above the daisies and floated beside a small apple tree. His eyes grew wide with surprise. "Lila! I'm flying!"
>
> *Toto:* "Lila! I'm flying!"

**Page 10 — The Windy Hill**
> Soon, Lila and Toto reached the top of a small hill. A gentle breeze brushed through the grass. Toto looked at the open sky, took a deep breath, and remembered what Lila had taught him: bravery doesn't mean you aren't scared. It means you try anyway.

**Page 11 — Toto Takes Flight**
> Toto spread his wings. He pushed gently against the ground and lifted into the air. Up he went, higher than the apple tree, higher than the castle wall, and higher than he had ever imagined!

**Page 12 — Finding Home**
> From high above the hills, Toto recognized a familiar little forest. "That's where I live!" he cried. Lila smiled as they followed the winding path toward his home.
>
> *Toto:* "That's where I live!"

**Page 13 — A Happy Goodbye**
> Toto's family rushed out to greet him. His little dragon mother hugged him tightly, and Toto proudly told them about his first flight. Before Lila left, Toto gave her a tiny green leaf shaped like a heart.

**Page 14 — Back at the Castle**
> When Lila returned to the castle, Queen Marielle was waiting in the garden. Lila told her mother about Toto and his first flight. Queen Marielle smiled and hugged her daughter. "Sometimes," she said, "the best way to help someone be brave is simply to believe in them."
>
> *Queen Marielle:* "The best way to help someone be brave is simply to believe in them."

**Page 15 — A New Friend**
> That evening, Lila looked out from her bedroom window. Far beyond the hills, a tiny green dragon flew beneath the golden sunset. Lila waved, and Toto waved his little wing back.

**Page 16 — The Lesson**
> And from that day on, Toto never forgot his first flight. Whenever something felt difficult, he remembered the little hop that started it all. Because sometimes courage begins with something very small—a single step, one tiny try, and a friend who believes in you.
