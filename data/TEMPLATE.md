## Core vocabulary

### Card: Premium vs sum insured
**Front:** Premium vs sum insured
**Back:** Premium = what the insured pays for the transfer of risk.
Sum insured = the maximum payable.

## Rules and remedies

### Q: A policy is cancelled mid-term and a short-rate refund applies. What does the customer get?
A) Exactly the unused proportion of premium
B) Less than the unused proportion, the difference recovering costs already incurred
C) Nothing — annual premium is fully earned at inception
D) The unused proportion plus a no-claims rebate
Correct: B
Rationale: Short-rate charges back more than a straight pro-rata calculation.
Why-wrong: A is the definition of pro-rata, not short-rate.
