# Princess Elara and the Secret Garden of Stars
*A Little Story About Kindness, Courage, and Wonder*

Ages 3–7 · 13 story pages · 8.5 × 11 in portrait

Narration is rendered **exactly as written** inside the cream storybook narration
bubble at the top of each page. Character dialogue goes in smaller pastel bubbles.

---

**Page 1**
> In a little kingdom of green hills and honeybees, there lived a princess named Elara. She loved the night sky more than anything in the world.

**Page 2**
> But one autumn, the stars began to go out. Each night, a few more quietly disappeared.

**Page 3**
> Then something small and warm tumbled into the roses. It was a star with one bent point, glowing very faintly.
>
> *Star:* "I couldn't hold on any longer."

**Page 4**
> The old gardener knew the story. Far past the thorn hedge and the singing river, there is a garden where stars are grown.
>
> *Gardener:* "It is a long way for small feet."
> *Elara:* "Then I should start now."

**Page 5**
> The thorn hedge was dark and scratchy, and Elara was afraid. So she went slowly, and she went anyway.

**Page 6**
> At the river, a moth lay shivering on a stone with a torn wing. Elara stopped to help, even though stopping cost her time.

**Page 7**
> She mended the wing with one silver thread from her scarf. The moth flew a happy loop and lit the path ahead like a tiny lantern.

**Page 8**
> At the top of the hill stood a golden gate with no handle and no key. Three words were carved upon it: Kindness opens me.

**Page 9**
> Elara did not know how to open it. Then the little moth landed on the gate, and it swung wide all on its own.

**Page 10**
> Inside grew the secret garden of stars, hanging like lanterns on silver vines. But the garden was dry, and tangled with weeds.

**Page 11**
> All night Elara pulled the weeds and carried water from the singing river. Then she tucked the little bent star into the warm dark soil.

**Page 12**
> By morning, the star had grown roots of light. And that night, the whole sky was shining again.

**Page 13**
> No one in the kingdom ever knew who saved the stars. But Elara knew, and so did one small moth.

---

**Ending Page**
> "And remember, little one:
> Even the smallest act of kindness
> can brighten someone's whole world."

**THE END**
