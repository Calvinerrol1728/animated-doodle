# Pinterest Pin — copy & paste

Two pin images, both **1000 × 1500 px (2:3)** — Pinterest's recommended size.

| File | Style | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `pinterest_pin_A_1000x1500.jpg` | Text panel on top, art below | **Start with this one.** Highest-contrast text, reads best in a busy feed |
| `pinterest_pin_B_1000x1500.jpg` | Full-bleed art with glowing overlay text | More atmospheric and "scroll-stopping"; better for repins and mood boards |

Pin both. Pinterest rewards multiple pins pointing to the same link — just space them
a few days apart and use different titles.

---

## Pin title (100 char max, ~40 shown in feed)

**Pin A:**
```
The Stars Are Going Out — A Bedtime Story About Kindness (Printable PDF)
```

**Pin B:**
```
"Kindness Opens Me" — A Bedtime Story for Ages 3-7 | Printable Kids Book
```

Front-load the hook. Pinterest truncates titles early, so the interesting words must
come first.

---

## Pin description (500 char max)

Pinterest reads this for search, so write for a *searching parent*, not a browsing one.

**Version 1 — story-led:**

```
One by one, the stars above Princess Elara's kingdom are quietly going out. When a tiny star falls into her roses, she sets out past the thorn hedge to find the secret garden where stars are grown — stopping along the way to mend a moth's torn wing.

At the top of the hill waits a golden gate with no handle and no key: "Kindness opens me."

A gentle bedtime story about quiet courage and kindness nobody claps for. 15 illustrated pages. Instant printable PDF for kids ages 3-7.
```

**Version 2 — parent-benefit-led:**

```
Looking for a calm, screen-free bedtime story? Princess Elara and the Secret Garden of Stars is a beautifully illustrated picture book about kindness, courage and wonder — perfect for children ages 3 to 7.

15 hand-painted pages. Print it at home or read it on a tablet. Includes 5 gentle discussion questions to talk through the story together.

Great for bedtime reading, preschool classrooms, and homeschool social-emotional learning. Instant PDF download.
```

## Hashtags (put 3–5 at the end of the description)

```
#bedtimestories #childrensbooks #kidsactivities #printablesforkids #readaloud
```

Pinterest treats hashtags lightly compared to Instagram — the description keywords
matter far more. Don't stuff them.

## Board & link setup

- **Board:** create one called **"Bedtime Stories for Kids"** or pin to an existing
  parenting/kids board. A niche board beats a general one.
- **Link:** your Gumroad product URL. Add it to the pin — this is the whole point.
- **Alt text:** `Illustrated children's book cover showing Princess Elara holding a
  glowing star in a moonlit rose garden.`

---

## Timing & strategy

- Pin **1–3 times per week**, not all at once. Steady beats bursts.
- Best times for parenting content are **evenings (8–11pm)** and **weekend mornings**.
- Pinterest is a **slow search engine, not a social feed.** Pins routinely start getting
  traffic 2–6 months after posting. Do not judge results in week one.
- Make **seasonal variants** later — a "cozy autumn bedtime story" or Christmas-gift
  version of the same pin often outperforms the original.

---

## Why these designs work

- **2:3 ratio.** Taller pins get cut off in feed; shorter ones get lost. 1000 × 1500 is
  the safe standard.
- **Text is huge.** Most Pinterest browsing happens on phones where a pin is roughly
  236 px wide. Both pins were tested at that size and stay readable.
- **The hook comes first, not the title.** "The stars are going out" makes someone
  curious. A book title alone does not.
- **A human face.** Pins with a clear character consistently outperform pure typography
  in children's niches.
