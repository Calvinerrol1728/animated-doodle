## Description

**Short version (Gumroad / Etsy first paragraph)**

A calm, complete system for organizing your money. Forty pages that take you
from your yearly overview all the way down to a single week's spending — and
back up again to a month-end review that actually tells you something.

Comes in four formats. Type into the spreadsheet and it adds everything up for
you. Type into the PDF and save it. Or print it and use a pen.

**Full version**

Most budget templates are either a bare spreadsheet or a pretty printable that
makes you do all the math. This is both.

The editable workbook has 18 tabs and live formulas throughout. Enter your
income and your spending; it calculates your totals, your difference columns,
your savings percentage, how much of each goal you have reached, what your
subscriptions cost per year, and how much you would keep by cancelling the
ones you marked. The dashboard pulls from every other tab automatically.

The fillable PDF has a form field on every writing line across all forty pages
— 1,600 of them — so you can type into it in any free PDF reader, save it, and
come back next month. Clickable bookmarks jump you to any page.

The printable PDFs are for anyone who thinks better with a pen. Clean tables,
generous writing room, thin elegant rules, and a neutral sage-and-cream palette
that does not eat your printer ink.

## FAQ answers

**Can I edit it?** Yes — two ways. The spreadsheet is fully editable and does the
math for you. The PDF has type-in fields on every line.

**Do I need Excel?** No. The workbook opens in free Google Sheets, LibreOffice
and Apple Numbers. The fillable PDF needs only a free PDF reader.

**Does it reset each month?** Duplicate the monthly tabs in the spreadsheet, or
reprint the pages you need. The Year At A Glance tab keeps all twelve months.

**Is it dated?** No. Use it starting any month, any year.

**Can I use it on my iPad?** Yes — the PDFs work in GoodNotes, Notability and
any annotation app.
