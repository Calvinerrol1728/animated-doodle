## Instagram caption

The first line is all most people see before "…more". Make it a hook, never a title.

```
She stopped to help, even though it cost her. 🌙

One by one, the stars above Princess Elara's kingdom were going out.

So she set off past the dark thorn hedge to find the secret garden where stars are grown.

On the way, she found a moth with a torn wing. Helping it meant losing time she didn't have. She helped anyway.

At the top of the hill stood a golden gate with no handle and no key. Three words were carved on it:

"Kindness opens me."

She couldn't open it.

But the moth she saved could. ⭐

A bedtime story about the kind of courage that's quiet — and the kindness nobody claps for.

15 illustrated pages. Printable PDF for ages 3-7. Link in bio 💫
```

## Facebook caption

Facebook rewards longer, story-style posts and real conversation. Ask a question.

```
The stars above Princess Elara's kingdom were quietly going out. One by one.

When a tiny star with a bent point tumbled into her roses, she set out past the dark thorn hedge to find the secret garden where stars are grown.

On the way, she stopped to mend a moth's torn wing. It cost her time she didn't have. She did it anyway.

At the top of the hill she found a golden gate with no handle and no key, carved with three words: "Kindness opens me."

She couldn't open it.

But the moth she saved could. 🌙

I wrote this one for the small quiet minutes before sleep — a story about the kind of courage that doesn't look like courage, and the kindness nobody ever finds out about.

15 hand-illustrated pages. Printable PDF, ages 3-7.

👇 What's the bedtime story your little one asks for again and again?
```

## What actually matters

- **Post the 4:5 portrait on Instagram**, not the square. It occupies more of the screen
  as someone scrolls, which is the single easiest reach win available.
- **Lead with the hook, not the book.** "She stopped to help, even though it cost her"
  earns a pause. "My new children's book is out!" does not.
- **Instagram suppresses posts with outbound links** in the caption. Say "link in bio."
  On Facebook, links are fine.
- **Reels outperform static posts** by a wide margin right now. Even a slow pan across a
  few book pages with soft music will beat a still image. Use the 9:16 file as the cover.
- **Ask a question on Facebook.** Comments drive distribution there more than likes.
- **Post consistently, not perfectly.** Three posts a week for a month beats one polished
  post.
