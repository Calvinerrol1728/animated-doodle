## 1. Product Title

Pick one. All are under 60 characters so they don't truncate in search results.

**Recommended (keyword-rich, benefit-first):**

```
Princess Elara and the Secret Garden of Stars | Kids Bedtime Story PDF
```

**Alternatives:**

```
Princess Elara — Printable Bedtime Story Book for Kids Ages 3-7
```

```
Princess Elara and the Secret Garden of Stars (Printable Kids Book)
```

**Why this works:** the front of the title carries the unique brand name, and the back
half carries the words buyers actually type — *kids bedtime story*, *printable*,
*ages 3-7*, *PDF*. Gumroad and Google both weight the title heavily.

## 2. URL slug

```
princess-elara-secret-garden-of-stars
```

hyphen-separated, keyword-bearing. Never change it after launch — it breaks your links.

### The stars are going out. One small kindness can bring them back.

One by one, the stars above Princess Elara's kingdom are quietly disappearing. When a
tiny star with a bent point tumbles into her roses, Elara sets out past the dark thorn
hedge to find the secret garden where stars are grown.

On the way she stops to mend a moth's torn wing — a small kindness, and a costly one.

At the top of the hill waits a golden gate with no handle and no key, carved with three
words: **Kindness opens me.**

She cannot open it. But the moth she saved can.

## 8. Extra: 3 upload panels beat 1

Gumroad lets you upload several preview images. Use the slots — conversion improves when
buyers can see inside the book before paying:

1. `gumroad_cover_2560x1440.jpg` — the hook
2. A 2 × 2 grid of four interior pages — proof the art is consistent throughout
3. A photo or mockup of the book printed and held — makes a digital file feel real

Keep all preview images the **same height**, as Gumroad recommends, so the carousel
doesn't jump.
