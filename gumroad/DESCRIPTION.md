## 1. MAIN DESCRIPTION (Gumroad product page)

**The stars are going out. One small kindness can bring them back.**

One by one, the stars above Princess Elara's kingdom are quietly disappearing.

When a tiny star with a bent point tumbles into her roses, Elara sets out past the dark
thorn hedge to find the secret garden where stars are grown.

On the way she stops to mend a moth's torn wing — a small kindness, and a costly one.

At the top of the hill waits a golden gate with no handle and no key, carved with three
words: **Kindness opens me.**

She cannot open it.

But the moth she saved can.

## Plain-text note

Gumroad's description editor handles basic formatting but not full Markdown tables. When
pasting the Main Description:

1. Paste the text
2. Re-apply **bold** on the headers using the editor toolbar
3. Rebuild the "Details" table as simple lines, e.g. `Format: PDF + JPG image pack`

Keep the line breaks between paragraphs — the white space is what makes it scannable.
