# 🎤 My Agency Game Plan 2026 — Presentation Script

**Total time:** ~12 minutes + Q&A
**Where:** Day 3 — Business Plan Showcase
**Tip:** Don't memorize word-for-word. Internalize the flow: *Why → Goals → People → Discipline → Commitment.*

---

## SLIDE 1 — Business Plan Requirements *(30 seconds)*

> Good morning, everyone!
>
> Before I walk you through my plan, let me share what this document has become for me. Over the past three days, this Business Plan was my working draft — after every session, every tool, every discussion, I went back and made it sharper. It helped me do four things: **clarify my goals** as a new Unit Head, **think hard about my recruitment strategy**, **apply the lessons immediately**, and **build a plan I can execute the moment I walk out of this room**.
>
> Everything on my checklist is done. Let me show you.

*[Cue: click to next slide with energy — this is your "opening bell"]*

---

## SLIDE 2 — My Agency Game Plan *(30 seconds)*

> Panel, good morning! I'm **[Your Name]** — and this is **My Agency Game Plan for 2026**.
>
> In the next ten minutes, I'll show you four things: **where we're going** — our Vision and Mission. **who I'm bringing on the journey** — my recruits. **how I'll find and activate them.** and **the weekly discipline that makes it all happen.**
>
> Let's begin with the destination.

---

## SLIDE 3 — VISION *(45 seconds)*

> *(Read the vision slowly, with conviction — then pause 2 seconds.)*
>
> "By 2030, we will have achieved sustainable growth and client success at scale — becoming the most distinctive and admired team in our industry for our innovation, quality, and people-centric culture."
>
> *(Pause.)* Notice — this vision is not just about numbers. It's about becoming a team that **people aspire to join** and **families trust with their future**. Every goal you're about to see ladders up to this one sentence.

---

## SLIDE 4 — MISSION *(45 seconds)*

> "We exist to develop successful financial advisors and future leaders who transform lives. We add value by empowering families to live with confidence, purpose, and financial freedom — through proactive and disciplined financial planning."
>
> For me, the key words are **"develop"** and **"transform."** I'm not just recruiting producers — I'm building leaders. And we don't sell policies; we give families **confidence and freedom**. That's the "why" behind every activity in this plan.

---

## SLIDE 5 — CORE VALUES *(60 seconds)*

> Our team runs on four values — we call it **CARE**.
>
> **C — Client Obsessed.** Clients first, always. We listen, we advise, we deliver on our promises.
>
> **A — Accountability and Integrity.** We own our numbers, we keep our word — even when no one is watching. Especially when no one is watching.
>
> **R — Resilience and Courage.** In this business you will hear "no." We bounce back from every "no" and show up braver the next day.
>
> **E — Empowerment and One Team.** We grow leaders, we lift each other up, and we win as one team.
>
> These aren't wall decor — you'll see them wired into my recruitment profile, my activation plan, and my weekly calendar.

---

## SLIDE 6 — GOAL SHEET *(75 seconds)*

> A vision without numbers is just a wish. So here's how I translated our Vision into three time horizons.
>
> **First, the 2030 North Star:** a self-sustaining agency of **25+ productive advisors**, serving **1,000+ families** across Cebu and Central Visayas — and leaders I developed, now running their own teams.
>
> **Second, my 12-month targets:** **12 recruits licensed** — one per month — **8 active producers** by December, **120+ team cases**, and **100 new families served**. Plus my personal commitment: **2 cases a month**, because a leader must lead from the field.
>
> **Third, my 90-day sprint:** my **100-name candidate list**, **30 recruitment conversations**, **3 recruits contracted and licensed**, and **6 personal cases**.
>
> This scorecard gets reviewed **every Friday**, adjusted **every month**, and shared with my team leader — accountability is built in.

---

## SLIDE 7 — Profile of Recruit *(60 seconds)*

> So who am I looking for? My ideal recruit is a **college graduate, 23 to 45**, with **1 to 3 years** in sales, service, teaching, BPO, banking, or real estate — people who already know how to work hard and talk to people.
>
> But here's what matters more than the résumé: an **entrepreneurial mindset** — hungry for unlimited income — someone **coachable, disciplined, and self-motivated**, based here in Cebu or Central Visayas, and either licensed or **willing to take the licensing exam**.
>
> And on the right is my non-negotiable: the **CARE fit checklist**. Client-hearted. Accountable. Resilient. A team player. **Skills can be trained — character can't.**

---

## SLIDE 8 — Sources *(45 seconds)*

> Where do I find them? Six sources.
>
> My **warm market** — family, friends, classmates, neighbors — that's the first 50 names on my list. **Client and COI referrals** — I will ask for two names at *every* client meeting. **Social media** — three career posts a week on Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn. My **community** — church, alumni, barangay and business groups. My **past career network** — ex-colleagues from BPO, sales, and teaching who want more out of life. And **AXA Discovery Days** — I'll bring two guests every run, and convert every attendee into a coffee chat.
>
> No excuses — the market is everywhere.

---

## SLIDE 9 — Recruitment Activities *(60 seconds)*

> Now, sources become candidates through *daily* activity. This is my recruitment rhythm:
>
> **Setting recruitment appointments — 30 minutes a day, Monday to Saturday, 10 per day.** That's the backbone, straight from the program.
>
> On top of that: **5 coffee chat invitations a day**, **3 career posts a week** targeting 15 quality leads a month, **2 referral names at every client meeting**, **2 Discovery Day guests per run**, **follow-up within 48 hours of every first meeting** — 100%, no exceptions — and **one career talk a month** in campuses and communities.
>
> Small daily disciplines. Compounded weekly results.

---

## SLIDE 10 — BREAK *(10 seconds)*

> And before I show you how I *activate* these recruits — let's take a 15-minute break. When we come back: the part most teams skip, and where I plan to win.

---

## SLIDE 11 — Activation Plan *(60 seconds)*

> Welcome back! Recruiting is only half the game — **activation is where teams are won.** Here's my 90-day activation system:
>
> **Week 1:** every new recruit submits a **100-name list**. **First 60 days:** one hour of licensing study support daily — licensed **within 60 days**. **Twice a week**, we do **joint field work** — eight joint meetings a month, because they watch me sell before they sell. Every recruit gets **30 minutes of one-on-one coaching weekly**, with a scorecard.
>
> The milestone that matters: **first case within 45 days of licensing** — momentum changes careers. Every **Tuesday** is team sales meeting and product training — 90% attendance. And once a month, a **CARE check-in** — because my target isn't just recruits... it's **80% still active at Month 12**.

---

## SLIDE 12 — STRUCTURED WORK WEEK *(75 seconds)*

> They say discipline beats motivation — so this calendar **is** the plan.
>
> **Every weekday starts at 8 AM with the team huddle**, then **10 prospecting calls** before 9. Mornings are for **clients and recruit interviews**; afternoons alternate between **joint field work, referral asks, and follow-ups**. I end each day with **30 minutes of study and networking**.
>
> **Friday afternoon** is sacred for the business: recruit coaching, admin, and my **weekly review and scorecard**.
>
> **Saturday** is team day — training in the morning, **Discovery Day** twice a month, personal clients in the afternoon.
>
> And **Sunday** — Sunday is protected. Mass, family, rest, and just 30 minutes on Sunday night to plan the week. This is the **people-centric culture** from our Vision — starting with how I treat my own time, and my own family.

---

>
> This is my Game Plan. This is my commitment. **I'm ready to build.**
>
> I'm happy to take your questions. *(Smile.)*

# 🛡️ Q&A PREP — Likely Panel Questions

**1. "Your targets are aggressive. What if you're behind at Month 3?"**
> "The plan has a built-in feedback loop — a Friday scorecard and a monthly review. If I'm behind on recruits, I check the top of the funnel first: invitations, conversations, appointments. I fix activity before I touch the goal — the deadline flexes, the standard doesn't."

**2. "Where will your first 3 recruits realistically come from?"**
> "My warm market — I already have a shortlist from my past BPO/sales network. Two are former teammates I've informally talked to. Discovery Day will be their formal introduction."

**3. "How will you keep recruits from becoming inactive?"**
> "That's exactly why activation is heavier than recruitment in my plan. The first 45 days are structured: 100-name list, joint field work twice a week, weekly coaching, and a first-case milestone. Activity in the first 90 days predicts survival at Month 12."

**4. "You're also producing personally — 2 cases a month. Isn't that a conflict?"**
> "It's the opposite — my personal production is my training ground. My recruits join me on real client meetings, so I model the activity I'm asking of them. A Unit Head who sells stays credible."

**5. "Why CARE values in recruitment? Isn't experience more important?"**
> "Experience gets someone productive faster — but values keep them in the business. A resilient, accountable, client-hearted person survives the hard first year. Skills I can train in weeks; character takes years."

# 🎯 DELIVERY TIPS

1. **The 3-second rule** — after every big statement, pause and count 1-2-3 before moving on. Silence = confidence.
2. **Own the numbers** — when you say "12 recruits, 8 active producers," look at the panel, not the screen. The screen is for them; your eyes are for them.
3. **The CARE moment** — on Slide 5, slow down. Values slides are where panels decide if you're a leader or just a presenter.
4. **Sunday line** — deliver the "Sunday is protected" line with a smile. It's memorable and shows maturity.
5. **Closing power** — the last line "I'm ready to build" should be your loudest, clearest sentence of the day. Then stop talking. 🙂

*Script length: ~12 minutes at a relaxed pace. Practice out loud twice — time yourself.*
